Tactile-Feedback Robotic Hand
Independent project
  • Built a two-modality tactile front end: FSR normal-force sensing through a 16-bit I2C ADC, and PVDF-film slip-vibration sensing through a mid-rail-biased ×51 amplifier stage.
  • Sized the amplifier gain by characterizing the sensor noise floor with an FFT on MCU-sampled data, after measuring that the unamplified slip signal fell below the ADC quantization floor.
  • Detected slip on the bench from the total variation of the signal over a 0.5 s window sampled at 5 kHz, separating idle from sliding contact by a factor of 1.7.
  • Ported an open-source MediaPipe / MuJoCo / Mink teleoperation pipeline (dora-rs) to Windows for an 8-DOF hand, tuning IK damping and update rate for stable tracking.
ESP32 · FSR / PVDF · Analog Front End · MuJoCo · Mink IK · dora-rs

Wideband Fully Differential Amplifier
Course project
Designed a two-stage fully differential amplifier under fixed constraints (5 V single supply, one ideal voltage and one ideal current source, 300 Ω input, 1 pF load). Applied a regulated cascode to the tail current source and used the input/output transformer turns ratios as design variables, reaching 38.63 dB gain, 120.6 MHz bandwidth, and 247.92 dB CMRR at 14.6 mW.
Analog Circuit Design · PSpice
